<Badge color="green">Client</Badge> Through ug-lib.

```lua
UgLib.SkillCheck.Start(difficulties, keys?) -> success, reason
UgLib.SkillCheck.Cancel() -> boolean
UgLib.SkillCheck.IsActive() -> boolean
```

`Start` MUST run in a thread.

<ResponseField name="difficulties" type="table" required>
  1 to 10 rounds. Each is a `UgLib.Enums.Difficulty` or `{ areaSize, speed }`: area 10 to 120 degrees, speed 0.1 to 1.0 degrees per ms.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="keys" type="string[]" default="{ 'e' }">1 to 8 lower-case letters or digits. Each round asks for one of them.</ResponseField>

| Difficulty | Area | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Easy` | 60° | 0.25 |
| `Medium` | 45° | 0.35 |
| `Hard` | 30° | 0.45 |

- Every round MUST be hit. A miss, a wrong key or a full turn without a press fails.
- Lua picks every area, key and speed, and counts a hit only when the needle could be in the area at the time of the press. A forged hit counts as a miss.
- `reason` is set when it ended without an answer: `Busy`, `Timeout`, `Cancelled` (Escape), `Replaced` or `Closed` (`Cancel`).

## Example

```lua client.lua
CreateThread(function()
    local Difficulty = UgLib.Enums.Difficulty
    local success = UgLib.SkillCheck.Start({ Difficulty.Easy, Difficulty.Medium, Difficulty.Hard }, { 'w', 'a', 's', 'd' })

    UgCore.Network.TriggerServer('my-heist:Lockpick', success)
end)
```
