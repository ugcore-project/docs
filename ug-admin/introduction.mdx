ug-admin gives your staff every tool they need. Every staff action runs through one server pipeline, whether it comes from chat, the console or the panel: access by ug-core group, a rank check so nobody acts on a higher rank, validated arguments, an audit row and a Discord log.

## What staff get

- **Drone noclip.** The camera rises off the gameplay camera and flies with smoothed acceleration. The crosshair targets what it points at: land there, delete it, freeze a player, board a vehicle or copy a `vector4`. See [Noclip and spectate](/ug-admin/noclip).
- **Spectate.** A satellite swoop onto the target, then an orbit camera with a live card. Arrows switch players.
- **Vision.** Every player on the map with a heading blip, plus name tags over heads.
- **Modes.** God mode, invisible and frozen players, owned by the server through statebags.
- **Big messages.** Announcements, warnings and private messages show as GTA shards.
- **Reports with a live chat.** Players ask for help, staff answer from a desk in the panel. See [Reports](/ug-admin/reports).
- **Jail and mute.** Time counts while the player is online, survives reconnects and ends on its own.
- **History and notes.** Every staff action taken on a player, and private staff notes.
- **Live control.** Health, armor, needs, exact balances, and items removed slot by slot.
- **Everyone at once.** Revive every downed player, bring everyone around you, freeze everyone you outrank.
- **Panel.** `F10` or `/admin`: a searchable player list, details, every action, reports and the audit log.

All commands are on [Commands](/ug-admin/commands).

## What server owners get

- Access per action, by ug-core staff group: [Configuration](/ug-admin/configuration).
- An audit log in `ug_admin_log`, searchable from the panel and posted to Discord when a webhook is set.
- The panel's look, noclip, spectate and shards: [Look and feel](/ug-admin/configuration-look).

## What developers get

Server events for every staff action, every report and every rating. See [API](/ug-admin/api).
