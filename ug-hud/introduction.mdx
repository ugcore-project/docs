ug-hud draws the player's HUD: status, needs, money, job, voice, speedometer, compass, clock and weapon. Each player opens the editor in game and changes everything: where each widget sits, its style, size and opacity, the colors, the units and the effects.

## What players get

- **Eight widgets.** Status, money, identity, voice, speedometer, compass, clock and weapon. Each one can move, hide, change style, size and opacity.
- **Presets.** Neon, Minimal, Classic and Sport change every style and the accent color at once, and keep where widgets sit.
- **Any accent color.** Nine swatches and a color picker.
- **Effects.** A red vignette and heartbeat at low health, warning pulses for low needs, money changes that float up, a blinking seatbelt light and chime.
- **Cinematic mode.** Letterbox bars and only the essentials.
- **Smart hide.** Full bars hide themselves until they change.
- **Share codes.** Export a layout as a code and import a friend's.

Layouts are saved on the player's PC, in client KVP. They survive reconnects and server restarts.

## What server owners get

- One `config.lua` with the defaults new players start from.
- Seatbelt with crash ejection and cruise control.
- Fuel from the native, a statebag or any fuel resource's export.
- pma-voice modes and radio channel when it runs.
- Money from the ug-core `accounts` module, needs from the `needs` module. Both optional.

## What developers get

Client exports to add custom statuses, such as drunk or nitro, and to hide the HUD during your own screens. See [API](/ug-hud/api).

## Security

The HUD only shows. Needs, money, jobs and death state stay on the server. Saved layouts and share codes are untrusted: the page keeps only known keys with bounded values.
