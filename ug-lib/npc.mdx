<Badge color="green">Client</Badge> Runs in your resource.

```lua
UgLib.Npc.Create(options) -> npc
UgLib.Npc.Remove(npcOrId) -> boolean
UgLib.Npc.GetPed(npcOrId) -> integer?
UgLib.Npc.Clear() -> count
```

<ResponseField name="options.model" type="string | integer" required>Model name or hash.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="options.coords" type="vector4" required>Position and heading. The feet stand at `z - 1`, so use the coords of a player standing there.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="options.distance" type="number" default="50">The ped spawns within this distance and is deleted beyond it. At most 500.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="options.animation" type="table">`{ dict, name, flag? }` or `{ scenario }`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="options.invincible" type="boolean" default="true" />
<ResponseField name="options.frozen" type="boolean" default="true" />
<ResponseField name="options.onSpawn" type="fun(npc)">Runs after the ped exists. `npc.ped` is its handle.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="options.onDespawn" type="fun(npc)">Runs before the ped is deleted.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="options.data" type="any">Kept on `npc.data`.</ResponseField>

- Peds are local to the player: other players see their own copy.
- They ignore events and never ragdoll, so shots and cars do not move them.
- `GetPed` returns nil while the player is too far away. Do not keep the handle.
- Your NPCs are deleted when your resource stops.

## Example

```lua client.lua
local clerk = UgLib.Npc.Create({
    model = 's_f_y_shop_mid',
    coords = vector4(73.9, -1392.9, 29.4, 270.0),
    distance = 40.0,
    animation = { scenario = 'WORLD_HUMAN_STAND_IMPATIENT' },
    onSpawn = function(npc)
        print('clerk spawned', npc.ped)
    end,
})

-- Later
UgLib.Npc.Remove(clerk)
```
